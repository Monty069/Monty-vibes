What is the profit target on the E8 Markets E8 Signature $100K account?

$6,000, with a $4,000 maximum loss (trailing (end of day)).

What is the profit target on the E8 Markets E8 Pro $100K account?

$8,000, with a $8,000 maximum loss (static) and a $2,500 daily loss limit.
