The5ers FAQ

How many account types does The5ers offer?

5: High Stakes (2-Step), High Stakes (Classic, pre-15 Sep 2026 purchases), Hyper Growth, Bootcamp (3-Step), Futures Day Trade (Futures). Sizes range from $2.5K to $100K.

What is the profit target on the The5ers High Stakes (2-Step) $100K account?

$10,000 in phase 1, with a $10,000 maximum loss (static) and a $5,000 daily loss limit.

What is the profit target on the The5ers High Stakes (Classic, pre-15 Sep 2026 purchases) $100K account?

$8,000 in phase 1, with a $10,000 maximum loss (static) and a $5,000 daily loss limit.

What is the profit target on the The5ers Hyper Growth $20K account?

$2,000, with a $1,200 maximum loss (static) and a $600 daily loss limit.

What is the profit target on the The5ers Bootcamp (3-Step) $100K account?

What is the profit target on the The5ers Futures Day Trade (Futures) $25K account?

$1,500, with a $1,000 maximum loss (trailing (end of day)).
