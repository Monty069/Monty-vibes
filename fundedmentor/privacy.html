Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

FundedMentor (“we”, “us”) runs fundedmentor.com, a planning and risk-management tool for prop firm traders. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Google account basics: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password or access to your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • What you enter in the app: your prop firm accounts, trades, settings and plans.
  • Trading data you enter: account size, trades you record or import, and floating P&L you type in. Automatic MetaTrader sync is not available yet; when it launches, it will read balance, equity and trade history with a read-only investor password and can never place, change or close trades.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs such as IP address and browser type, used to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep your data in sync across your devices.
  • To calculate your limits, plans, position sizes and alerts.
  • To send service emails about your account (for example sign-in or billing notices).
  • To improve the product using aggregated, non-identifying usage.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your Google data for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our infrastructure providers: Supabase (database and sign-in) and Vercel (website hosting). Payments, when enabled, are handled by our payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.

Your choices

  • You can view and edit your data in the app at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data by emailing support@fundedmentor.com. We delete it within 30 days.
  • You can revoke FundedMentor’s access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by row-level access rules so each user can only read their own records. When broker sync launches, investor passwords will be passed only to the read-only sync provider and never stored by FundedMentor.

Children

FundedMentor is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change. Questions: support@fundedmentor.com.
