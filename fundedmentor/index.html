The problem

Most challenges aren’t lost on bad trades. They’re lost on rules.

Daily loss, counted their way

Some firms count from your starting balance, some from day-start equity, some from whichever is higher. One wrong assumption and a normal losing day breaches the account.

A drawdown floor that moves

Intraday trailing, end-of-day trailing, locks at break-even. Your floor is rarely where you think it is.

Consistency rules that cap your best day

Hit the target in one great session and the payout can still be refused.

How it works

Three steps, about two minutes.

  1. 01

    Pick your firm and program

    Every program listed has its limits, sizes and phases checked against the firm’s official rules page. Choose the account you bought.

  2. 02

    Set your goal and markets

    Pass, first payout or monthly income. XAUUSD, NAS100, EURUSD or futures. Your usual stop.

  3. 03

    Follow today’s plan

    Risk per trade, trades left today and a check before every entry. Log trades by hand or import a CSV; every limit recalculates.

Features

Answers before the trade, not after it.

Daily loss left and your drawdown floor, using your firm’s reference point and reset time.

Lots for your stop, by target pace, compounding or your consistency cap.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is FundedMentor a prop firm?

No. We don’t sell challenges or fund accounts. We help you plan and track the challenge you already bought.

Is it connected to Ment Funding?

No. FundedMentor is an independent tool with no affiliation to Ment Funding.

Where do the rules come from, and are they accurate?

From each firm’s official rules page or help centre. Every value shows its source and the date we confirmed it (last checked 31 Oct 2026). If we can’t confirm a program’s rules on the firm’s own site, we don’t list it. Firms change rules often: if a value looks out of date, report it and we’ll fix it.

Do you need my broker login?

No. You log trades by hand or import a CSV. Automatic MT4/MT5 sync is coming later and will only ever use a read-only investor password.

Can it place or close trades?

Never. It calculates and warns; you trade.

Does it guarantee I pass?

No. It keeps you inside your firm’s rules. Results depend on your trading.

Is it really free?

Yes, everything is free during the beta, with no card required. A paid Pro plan for automatic sync and alerts comes later; the core loss-limit numbers stay free.

What happens to my data?

Your accounts and trades are stored under your sign-in and visible only to you. Email support@fundedmentor.com to have them deleted.
